# Technical Debt

Verified, stable current debt only. Do not record speculative improvements, missing future APIs, transient failures, or duplicate active tasks.

No debt entry has been promoted through a focused evidence review yet.

## Entry Requirements

Each entry must contain:

- status;
- exact current source/manifest/test evidence;
- concrete risk or maintenance cost;
- bounded resolution path;
- owning architecture chunk when applicable.

Keep unresolved choices in `OPEN_DECISIONS.md`, future ideas in `TODO.md`, active execution in `TASKS.md`, and transient findings in `.artifacts/**`.
